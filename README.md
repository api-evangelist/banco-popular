# Popular (Banco Popular) (banco-popular)

Popular, Inc. (NASDAQ: BPOP), operating as Banco Popular, is a publicly traded financial holding company founded in 1893 and headquartered in Hato Rey, San Juan, Puerto Rico. It is the largest bank in Puerto Rico and operates through two chartered depository segments: Banco Popular de Puerto Rico (BPPR), serving Puerto Rico and the U.S. Virgin Islands, and Popular Bank (formerly Banco Popular North America), a New York state-chartered commercial bank serving mainland U.S. markets.

## Open Finance / API Posture

Popular does **not** publish a first-party public developer portal or downloadable API specifications for its U.S. institutions (popular.com). The subdomains `developer.popular.com`, `developers.popular.com`, and `api.popular.com` do not resolve, and no "Developers" or "APIs" surface was found on the public site.

- **First-party developer portal:** None found (aggregator-only model).
- **Downloadable OpenAPI/Swagger:** None published.
- **FDX (Financial Data Exchange):** No documented membership or FDX-conformant data-access API found.
- **CFPB Section 1033:** No published data-access / Personal Financial Data Rights program found. As a U.S. FDIC-insured depository institution, Popular is subject to the emerging rule, but no first-party posture is documented.
- **Aggregator access:** Consumer-permissioned account and transaction data is available indirectly through third-party aggregators such as **Plaid**, **Tink**, and **Finicity**, not through a direct bank API.

> Disambiguation: the open-banking portal at `apiportal.popularenlinea.com` belongs to **Banco Popular Dominicano / Grupo Popular**, a distinct Dominican Republic company — **not** Popular, Inc. (popular.com). It is deliberately not attributed here.
